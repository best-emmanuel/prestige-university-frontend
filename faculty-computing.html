Faculty Overview

Learn technology. Build solutions. Create impact.

The Faculty of Computing & Information Technology provides a rigorous environment for students who want to understand technology and use it to solve meaningful problems.

Students develop strong foundations in computing, software development, information systems, data, cybersecurity and emerging digital technologies while gaining practical experience through projects and collaborative learning.
